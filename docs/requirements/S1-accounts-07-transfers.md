# S1.7. Transfers

7. **Transfers** between two of a user's accounts are matched (same amount, a few days apart),
   so they count neither as spending nor as income, nor as a buy or sell of cash. Opposite
   amounts of cash, not trades, in two of the owner's accounts within 4 days of each other
   are linked as a transfer, the closest in date, however they came to match (made,
   imported, a day or amount corrected, a half freed when its partner goes); when two are
   equally close, neither is, and the owner links one by hand. One who writes two owners'
   accounts (a joint account shared to a spouse, and the spouse's own) may link halves of
   both by hand; an owner who doesn't read the other account sees the half as a transfer
   with an account they don't see, never which, and may undo it. Cash taken out of a bank account pairs this way with
   its deposit in a `cash` account, and money sent to a payment app with its arrival there.
   1. **Shares, funds or bitcoin moved between the owner's accounts** (two brokerages, a
      wallet and an exchange, two wallets): a transfer out and a transfer in of the same
      instrument and quantity (bitcoin's less the network fee), within days, are
      **proposed** as a pair, on each account's Overview, and the owner confirms or rejects
      it; a rejected pair isn't proposed again. Between two of the owner's wallets both sides
      are one transaction on the chain: they pair by its transaction id, whatever their dates,
      and two different transaction ids never pair; the days apply where a side has no
      transaction on the chain (an exchange's or a brokerage's line). A change of date, such
      as a time zone correction re-dating a wallet's transactions (S14.9.3), never changes a
      pair confirmed or proposed. Once confirmed, they arrive with the **lots
      they left with**, worked out from the account they left whenever its lots change:
      their basis, the day each was acquired, a wash sale's holding period, the fee aside
      (S8.19), so nothing is missing a basis and nothing is taxed. A transfer in left
      unmatched takes the lots typed with it (S2.2), else stays missing its basis and its
      day bought (S8.4).
   2. **In a budget**, a transfer between one of its accounts and one it doesn't count is
      money leaving or entering it, and counts by its category (S7.3.2).
