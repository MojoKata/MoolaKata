# Pair a payback

A deposit paired with spending awaiting being paid back: that much of the deposit pays it back, so
it isn't income, and the spending stops being awaited.

## Deposits that may pay it back {#deposits}

Money that came in, not a transfer, within 90 days after the spending, with some of it not yet
pairing other spending. The amount awaited comes first.

## Pays back

All of the amount awaited, or part. One deposit can pay back several: an expense report paid in one
transfer.
