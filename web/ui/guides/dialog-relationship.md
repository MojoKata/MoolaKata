# A Relationship

How you are related to one of the household's people. You add relationships you are in; an admin
adds any. Mothers, grandparents, cousins and in-laws are worked out from these.

## Person

For an admin: whose relationship it is. Anyone else adds and changes only their own.

## I am the {#relation}

Spouse, couple, parent, child, friend, colleague, or family (with a label). At most one spouse at a
time, and no one is their own ancestor. A spouse counts for wash sales on the date of each sale, and
for a joint tax book on the last day of its year, once both people have confirmed the marriage: the other confirms from their own Profile, and an admin
confirms for someone who isn't a user. Until then it counts for gifts only, and a change to it is
confirmed again.

## Other person {#other-person}

One of the household's people.

## From {#from-date}

When it began. Optional.

## To {#to-date}

When it ended; empty, it still holds. A divorce is the marriage's end date; a death ends a marriage
on that day by itself. The same two people can marry again: a new relationship.

## Label

For family: godmother, aunt by marriage.

## Remove

Removes a relationship entered by mistake. One that ended keeps its dates instead, so past years
show that year's family.
