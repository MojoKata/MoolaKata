# Account: Goal

The beneficiary's education projection: what the 529 may hold when school starts, against what the
years of school will cost.

![A 529's Goal](shots/account-goal.png)

Choose the beneficiary in the account's Settings to project their education.

## Funded

The median balance when school starts (Sep 1) against what its years will cost then, as a
percentage, and the monthly saving that would fund it fully, or that none reaches it by then.

## The chart

The balance by year, from the 10th to the 90th percentile of many runs, falling as each year of
school is paid. A model, not a forecast. Under it, what it is worked out from: the years of school
and the first of them, the cost per year in today's dollars (the amount typed, or a shipped average),
and the education inflation it rises by each year, from your assumptions.

## Its Years

What each year of school is projected to cost, rising with education inflation.

Change its figures, make it active, or share it on the Projections screen.
