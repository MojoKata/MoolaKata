# A Donation

## Type {#kind}

**Cash**, **goods** at their fair value, **appreciated shares** from a lot, a **QCD** from an IRA
(counted, but not deducted), money **into a donor-advised fund**, or a **grant** from one (not
deducted again).

## Organisation

Who received it.

## Date

The day it was given.

## Donor-advised fund {#fund}

The fund it went into, or that made the grant.

## From account {#from-account}

Where the money or shares came from: its side is recorded too. For cash, its withdrawal is
recorded, categorised Giving. Leave it for a gift already in an imported account.

## From lot {#lot}

The lot the shares come from. They leave the account without realizing a gain.

## Shares {#quantity}

How many shares were given.

## Amount

What was given: the cash, the goods' fair market value, or the shares' market value on the day
given. No gain is realized on donated shares: held over a year, the deduction is their market
value; held a year or less, it is their basis.

## What was given {#description}

The goods: two bags of clothes, a piano. Goods over $500 in a year need Form 8283; an item over
$5,000 needs a qualified appraisal.

## Paid by the fund

A grant from a donor-advised fund is pending until the fund pays it.

## Acknowledgement received

The organisation's written acknowledgement, needed for any single gift of $250 or more: the dialog
says so once the amount reaches $250.

## Acknowledgement

The acknowledgement letter or email, kept as a receipt.
