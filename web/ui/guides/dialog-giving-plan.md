# Giving Plan

The year's giving target.

## Target {#target-kind}

A percentage of income, or a fixed amount.

## Percent of income {#percent}

10% is a common choice.

## Income {#income-basis}

What the percentage is of: the year's income transactions (take-home pay), or a gross income or AGI
estimate you type.

## Gross income {#income}

From your W-2, pay stubs or last year's return.

## Target amount {#amount}

A fixed amount to give in the year.

## Count the donor-advised fund {#daf-counted}

When money goes into the fund, or when it grants it out. Either way, a dollar through the fund
counts once.
