# Categories

The household's categories: one tree that every user files under, so a transaction's category is
the same in every budget and for everyone who sees it.

## Month

The month the budget's other tabs show.

## Budget

Which budget the other tabs show.

## Refresh

Works the month's figures out again at once; they're kept once worked out, and worked out again
on their own when anything they come from changes.

## Who changes what

Anyone adds a category, at the top level or under one, and renames any. A category's parent, kind
and tax tag are its maker's to change until something is filed under it; after that, and for a
shipped category or someone else's, they're an admin's. A category nothing uses is deleted by its
maker or an admin. One in use is **merged** into another by an admin instead: everything filed
under it moves across. Every change shows in Activity, for everyone.

A few shipped categories, _Gifts_, _Giving_, _Cash_, _Fees and charges_ and the loan payments',
are counted by MoolaKata's own rules: they can be renamed, but keep their kind and stay.

How long receipts in each category are kept is set in Settings › Storage.
