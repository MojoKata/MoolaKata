# Move money

An amount put from one category to another for the month shown: covering an overspent category from
one with money left. In an envelope budget, money can also move from or to **To Budget**.

## From

A category or a reserve with money left this month, the most first: an emergency fund covering a
hospital bill. In an envelope budget, **To Budget** too.

## To

The category that needs it.

## Amount to move

For this month only. Where the line rolls over, or is a reserve, the move carries on with what is
left.

## Note

Why, for whoever reads the month later.
