# Record a Corporate Action

What a company did to the shares you hold: their lots are reshaped or moved to the new shares with
the days they were held, never sold and bought again. Delete either side of one on its transactions
and both go.

## Holding

The shares it happened to, in one account.

## Action

**Split or reverse split**: more or fewer shares, the same basis. **Symbol change**: the same shares
under a new symbol. **Spin-off**: new shares of another company, with part of the basis. **Merger**:
the acquirer's shares, cash, or both, in place of yours.

## Date

The day it took effect.

## New symbol

The new company's or the new name's symbol. A merger paid all in cash has none: it is a sale.

## New shares for each held

How many new shares each one you held became: 2 for a 2-for-1 split, 0.1 for a 1-for-10 reverse,
0.3333 for one new share for every three.

## Basis to the new shares

For a spin-off, the share of your basis that moves to the new company, in percent, as the company's
Form 8937 states it; the rest stays with the shares you held.

## Cash for each share held

What a merger paid in cash for each share, besides any shares. It is asked of every merger: for one
paid all in shares, **Min** fills in $0.00. Cash in lieu of a fractional share is a sale of its own:
record it as one.

## A new share's price that day

With cash and shares, the cash is a gain only as far as the gain goes (IRC §356), and the price of
the acquirer's share that day tells the gain. The gain shows on Form 8949; the new shares' basis is
the old less the cash, plus that gain.
