## Manual

What has no free ticker (a 401(k)'s collective trust, a single bond) is valued from a typed price or
a statement value, with its date. A statement value is stale once the account's next statement is
overdue, or after a year and 60 days in an account that expects none; a typed price is stale after
90 days. A typed price is its typer's: it values their own accounts, and another person's only when
they type one too.
