# Cash Flow

The month's income on the left flowing into what it was spent on, by top-level category, and what
was saved. Only categorised transactions in the budget's accounts count; transfers between your
accounts don't.

A reserve is listed here once it has an account it's held in, given with **Edit Plan**; a card once
it is among the budget's accounts.
