# An Organisation

## Name

What it's called.

## EIN

Its employer identification number, from its receipts or the IRS's list of charities: two digits,
a hyphen and seven more (36-3136294). Blank, it has none recorded.

## Deductible

Gifts to a 501(c)(3) charity are deductible; to a political campaign, a GoFundMe or a person they
aren't.

## Causes

Tags, separated by commas, for giving by cause: Faith, Hunger, Local.

## Pledge

An amount promised, shown as expected donations until the year's end. It is due from the 25th of
each month (December 1 for a yearly one), counted from the month or year you set it in: a month
missed stays due, and a later gift pays the oldest first. Changing the amount or how often starts
the count again; clearing it ends the pledge.

## Every {#pledge-every}

A month or a year.

## Notes

Anything else.
