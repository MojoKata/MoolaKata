# New user

They get an invitation by email and choose their own password.

## Name {#display-name}

As others see it.

## Sex

Their person's (every user is one): it chooses the words for the family tree. Someone
already among the household's people under that name becomes this user.

## Username

What they sign in with: lowercase letters, digits, dots, dashes or underscores.

## Email address {#email}

Where the invitation goes.

## Groups

Their groups. **Admins** makes them an admin, who manages users, groups and Settings.
