# Sign-Up

Whether people can ask for an account themselves, from the sign-in screen.

## Sign-up

When on, the sign-in screen offers **Sign up**. A person gives their name, email, a username and a
password, confirms the email from a link, and waits; the request then shows under **Requests** in
Users and Groups, where an admin approves or rejects it. It needs email (SMTP) and the public URL,
which the links are built from.

## Allowed email domains {#signup-domains}

Optional: only addresses at these domains can ask, separated by commas. Requests from other
domains are refused. Empty allows any.
