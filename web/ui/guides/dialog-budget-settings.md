# Budget settings

A budget's name, its options and its accounts, and where its money comes from. It keeps its method.

## Name

Such as _Household_.

## Reserves

Save toward a target in a category, month by month. Turned off, the reserves' lines stay in the plan
as ordinary lines.

## Moves

Cover one category from another for a month. An envelope budget always has moves.

## Accounts

The accounts whose transactions count. Only the budget's owner chooses them. An account left out that
often gets transfers from these is named, so you can include it.

## Paid from {#paid-from}

The account a card is paid from. Nothing to set: it's learned from the card's payments, so a card
each for two spouses, paid from different accounts, just works. Choose one only where the history is
mixed.

## Moved from {#moved-from}

Where money for the reserves held in a savings account moves from, which **Where the money is**
suggests. It's proposed from the account that sends this one the most, and is only a suggestion: a
transfer from any of the budget's accounts counts the same.
