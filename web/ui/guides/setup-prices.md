# Price Sources

The welcome's last step: the price sources that need no key. Each is off until you turn it on, and
each one on calls the hosts under its name once a day, after the US market close. Turn them on here
or later in Settings › Prices, where sources that need a free key are added too.

Until a source is on, nothing is priced: holdings are valued from the brokers' files and their last
trades, the header reads **No Price Source**, and the benchmark charts have no prices yet.

## CBOE delayed quotes

US stocks, ETFs and options: daily closes, and the day's close once the market has closed. Not an
official API: CBOE offers it for personal, non-commercial use.

## Coinbase

Major coins' daily closes, and gold's spot through a token backed by gold.

## Continue

Adds the sources turned on, and opens the Dashboard.
