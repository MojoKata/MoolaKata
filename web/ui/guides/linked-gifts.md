# Gifts

The gifts planned for a person shared with you, and the people shared with you on their own.
Everything here is read only and comes from that household's server; nothing is worked out again
here.

## People Shared with You {#people}

A person that household shares with you: their name, their birthday, and how they are related to
the others there you may see.

## The header strip {#strip}

Which server this comes from, that it is read only, when it last synced, and who unlocked it and
when it locks. **Lock Now** locks it for everyone at once.
