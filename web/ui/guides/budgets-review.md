# Review

Last month's loose ends, each with its action: what was overspent, what is to move, what is left in
To Budget, and what waits to be sorted.

## Month

The month the budget's other tabs show.

## Budget

Which budget to review.

## Refresh

Works the month's figures out again at once. They are kept once worked out, and worked out again on
their own when anything they come from changes.

## Month reviewed

The month this review is of: last month unless you pick another.

## Mark Reviewed

Records who reviewed the month, and when, with its figures then as each person the budget is shared
with sees them. A reviewed month whose figures later change (a late transaction, a recategorised one,
a move in it) is flagged **changed** to each person whose own figures changed, with what changed,
until it's reviewed again: a planned line's spending or plan, spending outside
the plan, the uncategorised count, what left or entered the budget, and each of the month's totals
(spent, income, saved, To Budget) that changed. Months are never locked.
