# Review

Last month's loose ends, each with its action: what was overspent, what is to move, what is left in
To Budget, and what waits to be sorted.

## Month

The month the budget's other tabs show.

## Budget

Which budget to review.

## Refresh

Works the month's figures out again at once. They are kept once worked out, and worked out again on
their own when anything they come from changes.

## Month reviewed

The month this review is of: last month unless you pick another.

## Mark reviewed

Records who reviewed the month, and when, with its figures then. A reviewed month whose figures later
change (a late transaction, a recategorised one, a move in it) is flagged **changed since reviewed**,
with what changed, until it's reviewed again. Months are never locked.
