# Tax Tables

Figures that change each year and ship with MoolaKata: contribution limits, the gift exclusion, the
QCD limit, the bitcoin models' published figures, and more. They are updated with MoolaKata; correct
one here if it changes before an update. A corrected figure is marked, and setting it back to the
shipped value removes the correction.

![Settings: Tax Tables](shots/settings-tax-tables.png)

## Year

The tax year shown. A year after the latest shipped uses the latest.

## Figure

Dollars, a percentage, a number, or several numbers separated by commas (a model's parameters, or
bear, base and bull targets). The federal return's due date is a month and a day, *4, 15* for
April 15. A Roth IRA phase-out is two dollar amounts, where it starts and where it ends, the end
above the start: *153000, 168000*.
