# Compare

The budget shown beside another, per category: what is available, spent and left in each, for the
month or the year to it.

## Month

The month compared, or the last month of the year to it.

## Budget

The budget on the left.

## Compared with {#compared-with}

The budget on the right: usually the active one beside a scenario.

## Range

The month alone, or the year to it.
