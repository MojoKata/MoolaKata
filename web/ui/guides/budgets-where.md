# Where the money is

For each account reserves are held in: what they hold, what was moved there, and what is to move
there or back. And for each card in the budget, what it owes and how it's paid.

## Month

The month whose figures are shown.

## Budget

Which budget's reserves and cards.

## Refresh

Works the month's figures out again at once; they're kept once worked out, and worked out again
on their own when anything they come from changes.

## Keep as the account's own {#keep-own}

A transfer into the account from the budget's accounts counts as money moved in for the reserves.
When it's savings of the account's own instead, keep it as the account's own: it then doesn't count
for the reserves, and **to move** no longer asks for it back.

## Unassigned

Money that landed in the account other than by a transfer from the budget's accounts: its interest,
a paycheck split into it, a transfer from an account the budget doesn't count. **Assign** gives it
to the reserves held there or keeps it as the account's own. Given to a reserve, it counts as moved
in, so nothing more is asked to move for it.

## To move

What the reserves gained since they began, less what was moved in from the budget's other accounts
and the landed money given to them. When the reserves are ahead, move the difference there from the
account it's moved from; after a repair paid from checking or a card, it goes back, summed into one
transfer for the month. MoolaKata moves no money: make the transfer at the bank, and once it's
imported and paired the account reads **backed**. Money of the account's own doesn't count, and a
warning shows before a move back would go past its **Withdrawals allowed a month**.
