# A Written Email

An email written to the mail folder while SMTP was not set up, as it would have been sent: who
it is for, its subject and its text, links included. Pass the link on to the person it is for
by a way you trust; it works once, until it expires: an hour for a password reset, a week for an
invitation, a day for the rest. Your password was asked again to open it, since the link signs
someone in or resets a password.
