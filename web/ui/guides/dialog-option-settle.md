# What happened to the option

## It was {#how}

**Expired worthless**: a short option keeps its credit as a gain; a long one loses its premium. **Assigned** (a short option): the shares are bought (a put) or sold (a call) at the strike, the credit taking off the shares' basis or adding to the proceeds. **Exercised** (a long option): the shares are bought (a call) or sold (a put) at the strike, the premium adding to the basis or coming off the proceeds.

## On {#settle-date}

The day it expired, or was assigned or exercised.

## Contracts {#settle-contracts}

How many, from 1 to the contracts held: **All** fills in every one.
