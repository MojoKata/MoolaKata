# Reimbursements

Spending marked **Reimbursable** in its transaction and not yet paid back in full, oldest first. It
keeps its category, and the budget doesn't count it while it's awaited.

## Month

The month the budget's other tabs show; this list isn't by month.

## Budget

Which budget's accounts to list.

## Pair a payback {#pair}

Pairs a deposit with the spending: that much of it pays the spending back and isn't income. A
deposit of the amount awaited is offered first. If it's never paid back, lower the amount in the
transaction, or turn **Reimbursable** off, and the rest counts again.
