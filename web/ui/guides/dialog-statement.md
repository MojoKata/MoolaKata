# Check the statement

What was read from the statement, before anything is saved. Nothing is saved until you import it.

## Period from {#period-start}

The statement's first day. The opening balance is the balance the day before.

## Period to {#period-end}

Its last day, the day of the closing balance.

## Opening balance {#opening}

As the statement shows it. A card's balance owed shows as a negative amount here, as the card's
account does.

## Closing balance {#closing}

As the statement shows it. The transactions must add up from the opening balance to this one.

## Include

Untick a line that isn't a transaction (a subtotal, a daily balance). A line already in the account
is ticked and can't be unticked: it counts towards the closing balance and isn't added again.

## Remember… {#remember}

A line you untick offers **Remember…**: it asks to ignore future lines starting like it (its first
three words, which you can change), from this institution's next statements on. They still show,
left out, saying so, with **Stop ignoring**. Profile lists what you ignore, to remove it.

## Description

The payee as the statement words it; payee rules tidy it after.

## Amount

Money in is positive, money out negative. A line whose sign was guessed says so: check it. Once the
transactions add up, **Adds up** says yes.

## Accept difference {#accept-difference}

Import even though they don't add up. The Reconcile tab then shows the difference, until it is found
or accepted there.

## Learning the layout

What you correct is remembered for the institution: which section means money in or out. Lines that
are never transactions are ignored only when you ask, with **Remember…**. The next statement from it needs fewer corrections. The statement
itself is kept as a receipt on the account.
