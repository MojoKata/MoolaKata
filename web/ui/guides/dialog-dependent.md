# A Dependent

Someone the filers claim for the year, from the household's people. MoolaKata doesn't decide who
qualifies: what you enter here feeds the panels, the documents expected and a few flags. Each
dependent's age on Dec 31 shows in the panels: under 17, under 13, and who ages out this year.

## Person {#person}

One of the household's people, with their relation to the filers on Dec 31. Someone not listed yet
is added on the Gifts screen first.

## Claimed as {#claimed-as}

**Qualifying child** or **qualifying relative**, as you file it. The family tree suggests it: a
child or grandchild under 19, or under 24 and a full-time student, is suggested as a qualifying
child; anyone else as a qualifying relative.

## Months lived with you {#months}

How many months of the year they lived with the filers, from 0 to 12.

## Full-time student

A full-time student for at least five months of the year. Their 1098-T is then expected, and shows
beside the 529 withdrawals for them in Education.

## Accounts in their name {#accounts-in-their-name}

The accounts this book counts that are theirs, such as a custodial account. A dependent child's
interest, dividends and gains in them are checked against the year's Form 8615 threshold from
Settings › Tax Tables.

## Remove

Takes them off this year's book only.
