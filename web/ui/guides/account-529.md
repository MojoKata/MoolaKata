> your net worth, not theirs.

The beneficiary, the plan and its state, when it was opened and when a Roth IRA rollover becomes

@@ settings

## The plan

The plan's state and name, the beneficiary, when it was opened, and the state tax deduction you
expect.

## Plan state {#plan-state}

The state that sponsors the plan, which may not be yours.

## Plan name {#plan-name}

my529, Bright Start, and so on.

## Beneficiary

Who it is for: one of the household's people. **Add a person…** opens **Add a person** over this: the one it adds is chosen here.

## Opened {#opened-on}

The day the account was opened: a Roth IRA rollover is possible 15 years on.

## State tax deduction a year {#state-deduction}

What you expect to deduct on your state return each year. Typed: state rules aren't modelled.
