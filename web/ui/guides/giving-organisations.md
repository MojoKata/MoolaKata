# Giving: Organisations

The organisations you give to, with what each received this year.

![Organisations](shots/giving-organisations.png)

Add the charities, churches and causes you give to, with their EIN, and any pledges.

## Year

The year shown.

## Plan

Shown when someone shares their giving plan for the year with you: **Mine** shows your own giving,
their name theirs. What you add goes into the plan shown; into one shared with you to read, you
can't add.

## Add an Organisation

A charity, church, school or cause, with its EIN, whether gifts to it are deductible, its causes and
any pledge.

## Organisations

Click one to change it. A pledge shows as expected donations in the year's projection.
