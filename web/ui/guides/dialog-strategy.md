# A Strategy

## Name {#strategy-name}

Your own name for it; empty, its shape names it.

## Cash-secured

For a short put: the cash to buy the shares is set aside.

## Margin requirement {#margin-requirement}

Your broker's, for a strategy whose loss has no limit (a naked call, a short straddle or strangle):
it is then its capital at risk.

## Ungroup

Undoes the group. Each leg stands on its own again, its lots, gains and history unchanged.
