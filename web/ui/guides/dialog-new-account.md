# New account

Choose the account's type. It decides the fields, the tax treatment and the tabs.

## Banking

Checking, savings, money market and CDs: cash, taxable interest. **Cash** is cash on hand, kept by
hand; a **payment app** is Venmo, PayPal, Cash App and the like. Money moved to either from a bank
account pairs with it as a transfer.

## Credit card

A liability: a balance you owe shows as negative.

## Brokerage

Stocks, ETFs and funds, taxable.

## Retirement

Traditional and Roth IRAs, 401(k), 403(b) and 457(b) plans, and HSAs: contributions record their tax
year.
