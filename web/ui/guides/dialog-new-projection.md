# New projection

It starts as a scenario, with figures to change; make it active to follow it.

## Goal type {#goal}

**Retirement**, for you or a couple: the chance the money lasts. **Education**, for one person: how
much of their years of school their 529s would pay for. **Savings target**: the chance of an amount
by a date.

## Name

Such as _Retire at 63_, _Emma's college_ or _House deposit_.

## Built on

A portfolio, an account, or a typed amount for a what-if.

## Amount {#typed-amount}

The typed amount.

## For {#beneficiary}

For an education goal: who it is for, from your list of people; add them in a 529's settings or on
Gifts. Its 529 accounts are a good thing to
build it on.

## Who

You, or you and your spouse (from your relationships in Profile): each with a birth year and retirement
age.
