# Make active

For a budget: the budget you follow from now on. The one before stays as a scenario. Nothing is
rewritten: each keeps its months, moves and leave-outs. The Dashboard, budget alerts and what to
move follow the active one.

For a projection: its median path and 10th–90th percentile band as of today become the baseline it
is tracked against. Another active retirement plan for the same people stops being active: its
tracking ends, and it stays as a scenario.
