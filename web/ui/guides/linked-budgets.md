# Budgets

The budgets shared with you, in tabs: **This Month** (its's plan and spending by category),
**Trends** (spending by category over the months), **Cash Flow** (money in and out by month) and
**Recurring** (the bills and income that repeat). Everything here is read only and comes from that household's server; nothing is worked
out again here.

## The header strip {#strip}

Which server this comes from, that it is read only, when it last synced, and who unlocked it and
when it locks. **Lock Now** locks it for everyone at once.
