# A Category

A category of the household's tree. Everyone sees it, and a change to it shows in Activity.

## Name

Up to 60 characters, without ›. Two categories under the same parent can't share a name, whatever
its case. Anyone may rename a category; everyone sees the new name.

## Under

The top-level category it goes under, or none for a top-level one. Categories have two levels, so
one with categories of its own stays at the top. Moving it changes which parent's plan line counts
it in a budget that plans the parent alone.

## Kind

How budgets count what's filed under it: **expense** and **income** count; **savings** counts as
money put away; **transfer** never counts. Changing it changes every budget's past months too.

## Tax tag

Puts what's filed under it in Taxes › Deductions, for every tax book whose accounts use it, past
years too. Most categories have none.

## Find Them

For a category in use: how many uses you can see, and how many you can't (a transaction counts
once, whatever its parts). **Find Them** opens the **Find uses** tour beside the page, listing each
one you can see with a link to it; its counts fall as you give them another category, until you
can delete it, or an admin merges it.
