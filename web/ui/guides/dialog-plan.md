# Edit plan

The plan from the month shown on. Earlier months keep the plan they had.

## Category

A category plans its children too: plan _Food_ for groceries and dining together, or each on its
own.

## Amount

What you mean to spend in a month, or a year for a yearly line. Income categories plan what you
expect to receive.

## Every

**Month** compares with each month; **Year** with the year so far, for costs that come once or twice
a year.

## Rollover

Carry what is left at the end of a month into the next, or what was overspent out of it.

## Overspent

In an envelope budget, what a category overspent at the month's end does: **carried negative** into
the next month, or covered **from next month's To Budget**.

## Target

Makes the line a reserve: it puts in its amount each month toward this, never more than reaches it,
and spending in its category takes from it. At the target it reads **Funded** and puts in nothing
until spending draws it down. Taking the target off asks first, since the balance stops being kept.
Shown when the budget has reserves.

## Held in

The account a reserve's money is kept in, for **Where the money is**.

## Projection

For a savings line, an active projection: its monthly contribution shows beside the plan, so you see
whether you put away what your plan assumes.

## Saving

Each line shows the plan as stored, never the month's figures with what carried over and the
month's moves, so saving without a change changes nothing. A line changed holds from this month;
**✕** takes it out from this month. If a reserve would stop while it holds money, saving asks
first. Closing with changes asks before discarding them.
