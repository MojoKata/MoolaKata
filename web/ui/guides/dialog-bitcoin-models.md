# Bitcoin Models

Published models of bitcoin's price, each from today's price on a log scale, with bitcoin's price
history behind them, and each one's figures for 2030, 2035 and 2045 and what is known of its record. They are models other people publish, not
MoolaKata's view and not advice: the curve fits describe the past, stock-to-flow has run below its
own band since 2022, and the targets come from people who hold bitcoin.

Their figures ship with MoolaKata, dated, in Settings › Tax Tables, where an admin can correct them.

With no bitcoin price on the server yet, there is nothing to start from: an admin sets up a price
source in Settings › Prices.
