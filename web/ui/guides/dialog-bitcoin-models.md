# Bitcoin models

Published models of bitcoin's price, each from today's price on a log scale, with its figures for
2030, 2035 and 2045 and what is known of its record. They are models other people publish, not
MoolaKata's view and not advice: the curve fits describe the past, stock-to-flow has run below its
own band since 2022, and the targets come from people who hold bitcoin.

Their figures ship with MoolaKata, dated, in Settings › Tax Tables, where an admin can correct them.
