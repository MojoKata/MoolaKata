# Welcome to MoolaKata

A new server's first visit: its master key, then the admin's account. Everything MoolaKata keeps
is encrypted under one master key, which is a recovery phrase of 12 words. You write it down;
nothing keeps it for you. MoolaKata asks for it after every restart, and nobody can recover it if
it's lost.

## Show My Recovery Phrase

Makes a new key and shows its 12 words. Every visit, and every browser, shows new words, and only
the ones you confirm become the key: whoever only looked at a phrase knows nothing of the key the
server keeps.

## Your Recovery Phrase

Write the 12 words on paper, in order, with the fingerprint. Keep two copies in two places, one of
them offline, and never with the backups. Nothing shows them again; **Verify Key** in Settings ›
Key storage checks a copy later.

## Words back {#words-back}

Three of the words, by their number, from your sheet. Four letters of each are enough. A wrong
one is refused: check the sheet. **Back** shows the same words again.

## Make the Key

Writes the key, opens the server, and goes on to the admin's account. From now on the server is
locked after every restart until someone enters the phrase.
