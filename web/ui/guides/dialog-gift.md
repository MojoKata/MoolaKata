# A Gift

A gift for one or more people and an occasion, from idea to given; or one you received.

## Status

**Idea**, **Planned**, **Bought**, **Given**, or **Received** for a gift someone gave you.

## For {#people}

Who it's for: one or more of your people. For a received gift, who it was from. Type a name to list them and press Enter to add one; people are added on their own gift plans.

## Occasion

Which of their dates, and its year. With no date, it's an idea, kept on their list.

## Description

What it is: a watercolour set.

## Thank-you sent

For a received gift: whether you've thanked them.

## Cost

What it cost. Linked to a purchase, it's that purchase's amount unless you type another, so one
purchase can pay for several gifts.

## Paid with {#paid-with}

The purchase that paid for it, from the last 90 days; one purchase can pay for several gifts. It's then categorised Gifts in budgets, unless
it already had a category.

## Given in money

Cash or a cheque, counted against the annual gift exclusion. Money given to one person above the annual gift exclusion in a year is flagged for
Form 709.

## Where bought {#store}

The shop or site.

## Link

Optional: a link to it, for an idea. A web address starting with `https://` or `http://`, up to
2,000 characters.

## Notes

Anything else.

## Receipts

A receipt kept with the gift, for a return or a warranty.
