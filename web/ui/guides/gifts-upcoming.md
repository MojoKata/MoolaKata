# Gifts: Upcoming

The next 60 days of the birthdays, anniversaries, holidays and other dates in your gift plans and
those shared with you, each with its budget, the gifts planned so far, and what's still missing.

![Upcoming occasions](shots/gifts-upcoming.png)

Occasions come from the people on the People tab: their birthdays, anniversaries and holidays.

## Next 60 Days

Each date with how far off it is. One closer than the reminder time on its person's gift plan is
marked *soon*; with nothing bought, it also shows on the Dashboard and raises
an alert. **Add** plans a gift for it; a gift's chip opens it.
