# Gifts: Upcoming

The next 60 days of everyone's birthdays, anniversaries, holidays and other dates, each with its
budget, the gifts planned so far, and what's still missing.

![Upcoming occasions](shots/gifts-upcoming.png)

Occasions come from the people on the People tab: their birthdays, anniversaries and holidays.

## Next 60 Days

Each date with how far off it is. One closer than the reminder time on its person's gift plan is
marked *inside the reminder time*; with nothing bought, it also shows on the Dashboard and raises
an alert. **Add** plans a gift for it; a gift's chip opens it.
