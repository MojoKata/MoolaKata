# Record an option trade

## Trade {#action}

**Buy to open** a long call or put; **sell to open** writes one, a short position: a negative lot
whose credit is its basis, realized at close or expiry; **buy to close** and **sell to close** end them. Expiry, assignment and exercise are
recorded on their own.

## Account

The investing account it's in.

## Date

The trade date.

## Underlying

The stock or fund the contract is on: MSFT.

## Call or put {#right}

A call is the right to buy at the strike; a put, the right to sell.

## Strike

The price per share the contract is for: 480.

## Expiry

The last day it can be exercised.

## Contracts

How many: each covers 100 shares.

## Premium

The price per share, as quoted: a contract costs 100 times it. Once it is typed, the dialog shows
the cash paid or received, fees included.

## Fees and commission {#fees}

What the broker charged. They come off the cash, and so out of the gain. The dialog starts at
$0.65, and **default** under the field puts that back; type 0 for none. Left blank, they're refused.
