# Positions: Gains

Realized gains and losses grouped, and unrealized ones now.

![Gains](shots/positions-gains.png)

## Show

All positions, or only stocks and funds, options, or crypto.

## By

Group by year, month, account, instrument, or short and long term.

## Year

One year's sales, or all of them.

## Realized

Each group's short- and long-term gains and losses.

## Unrealized

What open positions would realize if sold at the latest close, by account (or by instrument),
each lot's gain under its own term. A lot without its cost basis has no gain to count: it is left
out, and the line under the table names its instrument and how many such lots it has, within
each account when the table is by account. Add the
lot's basis on the shares moved in to count it.

## Unknown Term

The gain of lots whose day bought is missing, shares moved in without it, shown only when there
are some. Type each lot's day bought on the shares moved in to give it its term.
