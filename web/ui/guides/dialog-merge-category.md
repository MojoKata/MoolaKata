# Merge a category

Moves everything filed under a category into another, then deletes it: transactions, their
parts, rules, budget plan lines and moves, and a loan's whole-payment category, for every user. An
admin's, and it can't be undone, though Activity records what went where.

## Into {#merge-into}

Another category of the same kind, which the line under it names. A category with categories of
its own merges into another top-level one.

## Its Categories {#merge-children}

Where each of its own categories goes: across, as it is, under the category it merges into; or into
one of that category's own. One with the same name is chosen to start with. One that moves across
can't keep a name the other already has.

## Budgets {#merge-budgets}

The budgets you can see whose plans change. Where a budget plans both categories, it plans both
amounts in one line from then on, past months too: a yearly amount counts as a twelfth a month.
