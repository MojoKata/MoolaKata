# New budget

A budget plans spending per category over the accounts you choose. Choose how it works and what it
offers now; plan its categories next, from history or with **Edit plan**.

## Name

Such as _Household_ or _My spending_.

## Method

**Tracking**: a plan per category compared with what is spent, received and saved. **Envelope**: the
money in the budget's accounts is handed out to categories until none is left to hand out, and what
you charge to a card is set aside for its payment. A budget keeps its method; copy it as a scenario
to try the other.

## Reserves

Save toward a target in a category, month by month: car repairs, a roof. Turn it on or off later in
the budget's settings.

## Moves

Cover one category from another for a month. An envelope budget always has moves.

## Accounts

The accounts whose transactions count: usually your checking accounts and cards. Investment accounts
aren't offered. An account you leave out that often gets transfers from these is named, so you can
include it: a cash account, a savings account. Left out, a transfer to it counts by its category, or
waits to be sorted. To share a household budget, use **Share** on it afterwards and give a group
**Write**.

## Estimate from history

Proposes a plan line per category from its average over the months you pick, and the recurring
payments as lines of their own. You keep, change or drop each before it's saved.
