# A Statement of Several Accounts

A combined statement, such as UMB's checking and savings or a Schwab combined statement, split
where each account's part begins. Each part is checked against its own opening and closing
balances, one after another. Nothing is saved until every part is saved or left out; the PDF is
then kept once, on each account it filled.

## Account {#part-account}

The account this part goes to: the one whose last four digits its header shows, or one you choose
when none or several match. When no account ends in them, choose one, or leave this part out.

## Period from {#period-start}

The first day the part covers.

## Period to {#period-end}

The last day it covers.

## Opening balance {#opening}

The part's balance before the period: it becomes the account's balance the day before.

## Closing balance {#closing}

The part's balance at the end: it becomes the account's balance on the last day.

## Leave This Part Out

For an account MoolaKata doesn't hold, or one you keep elsewhere.
