# Transaction

A change that moves the realized gains of a year whose return is filed asks first, naming the
year: **Change it anyway** makes it, and the return may need amending. The tax book lists the change
in its Issues until an amended return is added or someone says none is needed. While an amended
federal return is being prepared, the year is open and nothing is asked.

## Account

The account it belongs to.

## Date

As the institution wrote it.

## Kind

What it is: money in or out, a transfer, interest, a fee; or for an investing account a buy, a sale,
a dividend, shares moved in or out.

## Symbol

The instrument traded, such as `VTI`.

## Quantity

How many shares or units.

## Price

Per share or unit.

## Amount

The cash effect on the account: − for money out (a buy), + for money in (a sale), after fees.

## Fees

Commission and fees.

## Cost basis

For shares transferred in: what they cost. Without it the lot is marked **basis missing**.

## Payee

Who was paid, or who paid you.

## Category

Where the money went, for budgets and taxes. **Make a rule** files this payee the same way from
now on, from what you import.

## Split {#split}

One transaction across several categories; the parts must add up to its amount.

## Contribution year

For a retirement account: the tax year a contribution counts for. One made before the April deadline
may be for the year before.

## Contributed by {#contribution-source}

You, your employer (a match), or a rollover.

## Memo

A note.

## Tags

Your own, separated by commas; others who share the account don't see them.

## Reimbursable

Spending someone pays back: an employer's expense, a roommate's half. It keeps its category, for taxes
and reports, and budgets don't count it while it's awaited. It's listed under **Reimbursements** on
Budgets, where a deposit that may pay it back is offered to pair; the paired part of the deposit
isn't income.

## Reimbursable amount

All of it unless you type less. Lower it to what was paid back, or turn **Reimbursable** off, and the
rest counts as spending again, in the month it was spent.

## Owed by

Who pays it back, as you want to see it: _Acme Corp_, _Sam_.

## Leave out {#leave-out}

Each budget counting this transaction, and what it counts as there. **Leave out** is that budget's
own, for a one-off such as a car bought outright: it then counts nowhere in that budget, and
Activity records it. The category is the transaction's, the same in every budget.

## Receipts

The receipts attached to it; each opens its file. **Add a receipt** uploads one or more and attaches
them here. Whoever can read this account can see its receipts.

## Add a receipt {#add-receipt}

Upload PDFs or photos and attach them to this transaction at once.
