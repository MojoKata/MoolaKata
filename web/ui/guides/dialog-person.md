# A Gift Plan

Your plan for someone you give gifts to: your budget, their holidays and dates, and your notes.
Yours alone unless you share it.

A user of a linked server who may be this person can be joined: the linked server's user is then
this person here, who stays as private as now.

## The person {#the-person}

Name, sex and birthday belong to the person, seen by the whole household unless whoever added them
keeps them private. Once they sign in here, only they and an admin change them; until then, whoever
added them or an admin. How they're related to you is worked out from relationships. Gifts for
someone who uses this server are never shown to them, even through a share. A person already here,
chosen for a new plan, has their details changed once the plan is saved.

A user of a linked server is a person of their own here. When one has this person's name, the plan
offers **Join Them**: joined, that user is this person, which stays as private as it was.

## Name

One person, once: a name already here shows that person instead, to plan for them, or to ask whoever
added them to share them. **A Different Person** with the same name is added once both have birth
years, and they differ.

## Sex

Chooses the words for the family tree: mother or father, niece or nephew.

## Birthday

Their birthday as `YYYY-MM-DD`, with the year if you know it (for their age): 2018-10-20; or
without it: 10-20.

## Died on {#died-on}

The day they died. It ends a marriage on that day, so a widow or widower shows in the family tree;
their gifts stay in your history.

## Private

Seen only by you and those you share them with. Only whoever added them makes them private.

## Their relationships {#their-relationships}

Their family and friends, entered and worked out, as of today.

## Yearly gift budget {#gift-budget}

What you mean to spend on them in a year, all occasions together.

## Holidays

The holidays they get gifts for. Dates are worked out each year: fixed ones, rule-based ones like
Mother's Day, and lunar or lunisolar ones like Diwali from shipped tables.

## Anniversaries and other dates {#dates}

Any anniversary other than a wedding, a name day, any other date of their own, each with what it's
called, its day (`YYYY-MM-DD`, or `MM-DD` without the year) and a budget of its own if you like. A
wedding anniversary comes from their marriage.

## Remind me {#lead-days}

How long before a date it shows on the Dashboard and raises an alert, until something is bought.

## Notes

Sizes, likes, what they already have.

## Given Before

What you gave them in past years, so it isn't given twice.

## Delete Plan

Deletes your gift plan for them; they stay among the household's people.

## Delete Person

Deletes them for everyone, asking first. Gifts bought, given or received stay in the history as
_Emma (deleted)_; ideas and planned gifts for them alone go with them. A user's own person isn't
deleted here.
