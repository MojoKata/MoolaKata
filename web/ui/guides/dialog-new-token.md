# New Access Token

A token for an AI assistant or another tool that speaks MCP. It reads, never changes, and only what
you choose here. Making one asks for your password again if you gave it over 10 minutes ago, and
emails you; changing or resetting your password revokes every token.

## Token name

So you can tell your tokens apart (*Claude on my laptop*), and see each one's calls in Activity.

## Can read

**Everything I can see**: your accounts and what is shared with you, as it is at each call, so a
share removed later is no longer readable. **Chosen items**: only the accounts and portfolios you
tick. Never secrets, Settings or linked households.

## Chosen items {#chosen-items}

The accounts and portfolios the token may read.

## Expires

In 30 days, 90 days or a year. Revoke a token at any time.
